Graduate Futures: Tackling Youth Unemployment Among Social Sciences Graduates

A Social Impact Initiative by Social Scientist Sanctuary


About the Initiative

Graduate Futures is a social impact initiative focused on addressing the challenges faced by social sciences graduates as they transition from university into meaningful careers.

Social scientists play a critical role in understanding communities, shaping policies, supporting human development and creating solutions to social challenges. However, many graduates experience barriers including limited career pathways, skills gaps, lack of professional networks and challenges accessing opportunities.
This initiative aims to create conversations, resources and collaborations that empower social science graduates to transform knowledge into impact.


Our Vision

A future where social science graduates are recognised, supported and connected to opportunities where their knowledge and skills can create meaningful change.

Our Mission

To build a platform that connects social science graduates, professionals, organisations and communities to explore innovative pathways for career development, social innovation and impact.

Why This Matters

Social science graduates contribute to areas such as:

- Community development
- Social work
- Policy development
- Research
- Education
- Human rights
- Public health
- Sustainable development
- Climate Change

Yet many talented graduates struggle to find opportunities that recognise the value of these skills.

Graduate Futures seeks to bridge this gap.
