Graduate Futures

Tackling Youth Unemployment Among Social Sciences Graduates

A social impact initiative by Social Scientist Sanctuary exploring new pathways for social science graduates to transform knowledge into meaningful careers and community impact.

About the Cause

Social science graduates possess valuable skills in understanding communities, analysing social challenges, supporting human development and creating solutions. However, many graduates face barriers when transitioning from university into employment.

Graduate Futures aims to create conversations, connections and opportunities that strengthen the future of social sciences.

Our Vision

A future where social science graduates are recognised, supported and empowered to use their knowledge to create positive social change.

Our Mission

To connect graduates, professionals, organisations and communities to explore innovative pathways for career development, social innovation and impact.

Areas of Focus

  • Career pathways and professional development
  • Mentorship and networking opportunities
  • Social innovation and entrepreneurship
  • Knowledge sharing and collaboration
  • Transforming social science knowledge into action

Social Scientist Sanctuary

Graduate Futures is part of the Social Scientist Sanctuary vision: creating spaces where social scientists connect, collaborate and contribute solutions to social challenges.

Join the Movement

We welcome collaboration from social science graduates, universities, organisations, employers and social innovators who believe in creating stronger pathways from education to impact.
