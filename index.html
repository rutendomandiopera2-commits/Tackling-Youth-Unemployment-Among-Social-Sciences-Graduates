Graduate Futures

Transforming Social Science Knowledge Into Social Impact

A social impact initiative by Social Scientist Sanctuary focused on creating stronger pathways for Social Sciences graduates to move beyond the degree and into meaningful careers, innovation and community impact.

Why This Cause Matters

Social Sciences graduates bring essential skills in understanding people, communities and social challenges. Their knowledge contributes to areas such as social development, policy, education, research, public health and human services.

However, many graduates experience challenges transitioning from university into employment due to limited opportunities, professional networks and unclear career pathways.

Our Vision

A world where Social Sciences graduates are recognised as valuable contributors to solving social challenges and creating sustainable change.

Our Approach

  • Creating conversations about graduate employment challenges
  • Connecting graduates with networks and opportunities
  • Promoting mentorship and career development
  • Encouraging social innovation and entrepreneurship
  • Building bridges between universities, organisations and communities

Part of Social Scientist Sanctuary

Graduate Futures contributes to the wider Social Scientist Sanctuary vision: creating a collaborative space where social scientists connect, share knowledge and develop solutions for society.

Join the Movement

We invite social science graduates, universities, employers, organisations and innovators to collaborate in building better futures for the next generation of social scientists.
